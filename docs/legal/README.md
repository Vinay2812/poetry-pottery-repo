# Legal pages: source text

The Terms, Privacy, Shipping and returns, and FAQ pages are content pages edited in the admin (Content). This folder holds the text to paste there, so every published version has a dated copy in git history. The terms tell customers the studio keeps one.

- `terms.md`: the `terms` page
- `privacy.md`: the `privacy` page
- `shipping-faq-and-owner-tasks.md`: the `shipping` page, the FAQ answers to replace, the code-owned policy copy, and tasks that sit outside the pages

Format: `##` is a section heading, plain paragraphs are the section body, and `- **Title** — body` lines are section items. Square brackets are details still to fill in before publishing.

When you publish a change in the admin, update the file here in the same week and set the "Last updated" date in both places.

Reviewed by a two-model council (Claude Opus and GPT) against the Consumer Protection Act 2019, the E-Commerce Rules 2020 (with the 2026 amendment), the IT Act and SPDI Rules, and the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules 2025. This is not formal legal advice; have an Indian lawyer, and a CA for GST, check it before relying on it.
