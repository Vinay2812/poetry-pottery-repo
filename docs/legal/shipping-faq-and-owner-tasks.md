## Shipping

We ship within India only. Pieces from the shelf usually reach you within two weeks of payment, depending on where you are. Made-to-order pieces take three to four weeks to make; we agree the shipping date with you when you accept the sketch and price.

Shipping is a flat ₹150, free when your order comes to ₹2,500 or more after discounts. Checkout always shows the exact charge before you place the order.

Every piece is wrapped in paper, then bubble wrap, and packed in a double-walled box with the gaps filled. We do not offer cash on delivery.

If tracking says your parcel was delivered but it has not reached you, tell us as soon as you can and we will chase the courier. If it is lost on the way, we send the piece again or refund you in full, whichever you prefer.

## Cancellations, returns and refunds

You can withdraw an order from your orders page until you have paid. Once you have paid, an order cannot be cancelled, and we do not accept returns or give refunds if you change your mind. We do not charge any cancellation fee.

Every piece is handmade, so small differences in colour, glaze and shape from the photographs are expected and are not a reason for a return.

## If it arrives broken or faulty

Please record a short video as you open the parcel, starting with the sealed box. No video? Clear photos of the piece and the box are fine, and if you have neither, tell us what happened and we will look at it with you. Message us on WhatsApp or by email as soon as you can. For visible damage, hearing from you within 48 hours of delivery helps us claim from the courier. For a fault that only shows in use, such as a hairline crack or a leak, tell us when you notice it. Keep the packing until we have settled it.

If a piece arrives damaged, faulty, wrong or clearly different from what we listed or agreed, we will first offer to send a matching piece or make it again, and pay the shipping. If you would rather have your money back, we refund what you paid for that piece, including its shipping, within seven working days. You never pay to send a piece back.

If the outer box is clearly crushed or opened when it arrives, you can refuse the parcel, or ask the courier to note the damage before you accept it.

## If delivery is late

If your order has not reached you within the time we gave you (two weeks of payment for a shelf order, or two weeks of the shipping date we agreed for a made-to-order piece), you can keep waiting or ask for a full refund. If an unavoidable event such as a flood causes the delay, we will tell you what happened and give you a new date. You can still choose a full refund instead.

## If we cancel

If we cannot fulfil a paid order, for example because a piece broke before it left the studio, we refund you in full within seven working days.

The full terms are on our terms and conditions page.

- **How long until it ships?** — Pieces on the shelf usually reach you within two weeks of payment, depending on where you are. Made-to-order pieces take three to four weeks to make, and we agree the shipping date with you when you accept the sketch.
- **What if it arrives broken?** — Message us on WhatsApp or by email as soon as you can, ideally within 48 hours. An unboxing video or photos help; if you have neither, just tell us what happened. If a fault only shows in use, like a leak, tell us as soon as you notice. We offer a matching piece or make it again and pay the shipping, or refund the piece if you'd rather.
- **Can I return a made-to-order piece?** — Not for a change of mind: a piece made to your brief is final once you accept and pay. If it arrives broken or is not what we agreed, we make it again, or refund you if you'd rather.
- **Microwave and dishwasher?** — Most glazed stoneware is fine in both, and the top rack is kinder to it. Wood-fired pieces and any piece with a metallic glaze should be washed by hand and kept out of the microwave; the piece page says so. Hand washing keeps every glaze bright for longer.
