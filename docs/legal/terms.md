## The pieces

Every piece is thrown, trimmed and glazed by hand. Colour, size, glaze pooling, small marks and the exact shape vary from piece to piece and from the photographs, which show one piece from a batch. These variations are part of handmade work and are not faults. Every piece is made at our studio in Sangli, India.

Pieces sold as seconds came out of the kiln with a mark that we show or describe on the listing. That mark is part of the piece and is not a fault.

Glazed stoneware is made for food and drink. Most glazed pieces can go in the microwave and dishwasher; wood-fired pieces and any piece with a metallic glaze cannot, and the piece page or our care page says so. Avoid sudden changes in temperature, like a cold dish going into a hot oven. Fine crazing lines can appear in some glazes over the years; this is natural in stoneware. Unglazed terracotta is for plants and dry use. No piece is made for a stovetop, open flame or induction plate.

We do not offer any warranty beyond what is written on the piece page and your rights under Indian law.

## If a piece arrives broken or faulty

Please record a short video as you open the parcel, starting with the sealed box. It is the quickest way for us to see what happened. If you did not record one, clear photos of the piece and the box are fine, and if you have neither, tell us what happened and we will look at it with you.

Please message us on WhatsApp or by email as soon as you can. For visible damage, hearing from you within 48 hours of delivery helps us claim from the courier. For a fault that only shows in use, such as a hairline crack or a leak, tell us when you notice it. Keep the box and packing until we have settled it.

If a piece was damaged in transit, has a fault (a crack, a chip, a leak, or a piece that will not sit flat), is not the piece you ordered, or is clearly different from what we listed or agreed, we will first offer to send you a matching piece or make it again, and pay the shipping. If you would rather have your money back, tell us and we refund what you paid for that piece, including its shipping, within seven working days. If anything needs to come back to us, we arrange and pay for it.

If the outer box is clearly crushed or opened when it arrives, you can refuse the parcel, or ask the courier to note the damage before you accept it.

The variations described under "The pieces" are not faults, and neither is the mark that made a piece a second.

## Wheel sessions

- **Booking** — You request a date, time and number of people. We approve the request on WhatsApp and share how to pay. Your session is confirmed once we receive payment, and we send you a receipt.
- **Before you pay** — You can withdraw the request at no cost.
- **Once paid** — Your session stays on the date you chose. It cannot be cancelled, moved or refunded if your plans change.
- **Sending someone else** — If you cannot come, a friend or family member can take your place. Tell us their name before the session. If they are under 18, a parent or guardian must come with them.
- **Arriving late** — Please arrive ten minutes early. Sessions start and end on time. If you arrive more than 20 minutes late, we may not be able to seat you at a wheel, and the session counts as missed.
- **Missed sessions** — If you miss your session, it is not refunded or moved.
- **What to wear** — Clothes that can get clay on them, closed shoes, and long hair tied back. We provide aprons.
- **Your pieces** — We dry, fire and glaze what you make. They are usually ready in about two weeks; collect them from the studio or we can ship them for the usual shipping charge. We keep finished pieces for 60 days after we tell you they are ready, and remind you once before then. After that we may give them away.
- **Breakage** — We take care of every piece, but clay can crack while drying or in the kiln, and that is part of making pottery. If a piece is damaged because of how we handled it, you can ask for a refund or agree another way for us to put it right.
- **If a session is not as agreed** — If we do not run your session the way we agreed, you can ask for a refund or agree another way for us to put it right.
- **Safety** — Follow the instructions of the person running the session. If you need an adjustment to take part safely, message us before booking so we can talk about it privately. Please leave medical details out of the booking form. Wheels, tools and kilns can cause injury if misused.
- **Looking after each other** — Please be kind to the people around you and careful with the wheels and tools. We may ask anyone who is unsafe or disrespectful to leave, and the session is not refunded. Please keep valuables with you; we cannot look after belongings left in the studio.
- **Photos** — We sometimes take photos and videos in the studio for our website and social media. We ask before taking one where you can be recognised, and tell you where we would like to share it. You can take part either way. For a child, we ask their parent or guardian first. If you change your mind later, tell us and we remove it from everywhere we control.

If you hold a free seat and cannot come, please withdraw it or message us so someone else can have it. We may give unclaimed free seats away 15 minutes after the start.

At open mics, performers keep the rights to their own work. Please keep to the time you are given, and nothing hateful or unlawful. We may photograph or record the evening. We ask performers and guests before recording anyone who can be recognised, and leave out anyone who would rather not appear.

The lines on looking after each other and photos under "Wheel sessions" apply to events too, and so does the breakage line when we fire something you make. If an event has an arrival cutoff, we tell you before you book.
