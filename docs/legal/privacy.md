## What we collect

- **Your account** — Name, email address, and your phone number and profile photo if you add them. Sign-in is handled by Clerk. We never see or store your password.
- **Saved things** — Addresses you save, pieces in your cart and saved pieces, and the email address and piece for any "tell me when it's back" alert.
- **Orders** — The pieces you order and any options or personal touches you choose (such as text to add or reference photos), gift notes, the shipping name, phone number and address, notes you add, the updates and photos we post to your order, coupon codes, and the payment status we record. We record whether you have paid. When you pay by UPI or bank transfer, our bank or UPI app shows us the payer's name and a payment reference, which we use to match the payment to your order. We never collect card details, UPI PINs or bank logins.
- **Wheel sessions and events** — The dates you book, the number of people, notes you add, the name of anyone you send in your place (please let them know you have shared it), and the booking's status and payment.
- **Made-to-order briefs** — What you ask for, your name and contact details, and any reference images you send.
- **Studio visits** — Your name, phone number, the time you book and any note you add.
- **Reviews** — Your rating, words and any photos you add, shown with your first name.
- **Messages** — What you send through the contact form. When you tap a button on the site that opens WhatsApp with a message ready to send, we keep a copy of that message and the page it came from, even if you do not send it, with the name, email and phone number on your account if you are signed in, so we can follow up. When we reply to you from the site, we keep a copy and also send it to your email. Your WhatsApp chats themselves stay in WhatsApp.
- **Photos at the studio** — Photos or videos of you at sessions and events, only if you agree when we ask, shared where we told you. For a child, we ask their parent or guardian first. Tell us any time and we delete it or take it down.
- **Newsletter** — Your email address, if you sign up.
- **Search** — What you type into search is sent to our server to find results. Your list of recent searches stays in your own browser.
- **Technical data** — Your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you request, which our servers record to keep the site secure and working. If the server hits an error, an error report may include details of the request.

We only collect what we need for the purposes below. Please do not send us sensitive information such as medical details, ID numbers or bank details through the site.

## Why we use it

- **To do what you asked** — Confirming and shipping orders, approving and running sessions and events, replying to briefs and messages, sending restock alerts.
- **To keep you updated** — Emails and WhatsApp messages about your orders and bookings.
- **To keep records** — Keeping proper records of orders, payments and receipts.
- **To keep the site safe** — Preventing abuse, fraud and spam, and fixing errors.
- **Newsletters** — Only if you sign up separately. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

We use the information you give us for the purpose you gave it for, or with your consent. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

## How long we keep it

- **Account** — While your account is open. To close it, write to us; we delete what we can and tell you what we need to keep and for how long.
- **Orders, payments and receipts** — Eight years after the financial year of the order, to keep proper accounts and answer any tax or legal question about them. If you close your account, we keep only what those records need.
- **Bookings, briefs and messages** — Up to three years from the booking date or our last exchange about it, whichever is later.
- **Reviews** — Until you or we remove them.
- **Studio photos** — Ones you agreed to share, until you ask us to take them down. Others we delete within a year.
- **Newsletter** — Until you unsubscribe. After that we keep a note that you unsubscribed, so we never email you again by mistake.
- **Security logs and error reports** — Security logs for as long as Indian law requires (currently 180 days, and one year from May 2027), then deleted. Error reports for up to 90 days.

## How we protect it

The site uses encrypted connections. Only the people who run the studio can see customer data, and only through accounts with admin access. Our service providers protect data under their own security programmes.

If a breach ever affects your personal data, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.
