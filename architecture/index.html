Shopping

CatalogueThe shelf, the archive and filters that never hide their own options. SearchWhole words, prefixes, near spellings and meaning, ranked together. Made-to-order optionsChoices, words to carve and reference photos, priced on the server. Cart and wishlistInstant changes that the server confirms or rolls back. CheckoutLock, re-price, take the stock and write the order in one transaction. OrdersEvery status an order can move through, and who moves it. Back in stockThe waiting list, and one mail per person per restock. ReviewsWho may review, photos, likes and moderation. AccountOrders, bookings, addresses and newsletter for a signed-in buyer.

Studio sessions

Wheel sessionsAvailability, slot capacity and the hours picked ahead for you. EventsWorkshops and open mics: seats, registrations and their states. Studio visitsHalf-hour windows with one live visit each. CommissionsA brief, reference photos and the studio's reply.

Platform

Sign-in and identityClerk tokens, the guard and accounts created on first sign-in. Queue and mailJobs published after commit, retried, and dead-lettered. UploadsFrom a signed upload to a kept photo, or an expired one. WhatsApp recordEvery hand-off to WhatsApp, mailed and kept in the inbox. Content and settingsPages, settings, newsletter, contact and the sitemap. Admin consoleThe studio's console and the guards behind it.

Frontend

Data in the frontendServer-rendered first, the rest after hydration, writes kept optimistic. Storefront shellHeader, navigation, search panel and footer. Brand and designThe wordmark generator, icons and tokens.
